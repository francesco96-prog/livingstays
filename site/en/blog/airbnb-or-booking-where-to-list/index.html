Airbnb or Booking.com: where should you list your apartment?

Airbnb and Booking.com are the two main channels for short-term rentals in Italy. Since 2026 their commissions have moved closer, but their audiences and the way they work are still different. Here is how to choose — and why the answer is often “both”.

Commissions in 2026

Airbnb: the single host fee arrives

Airbnb is moving all hosts to the host-only fee model: the whole commission, 15.5% in most cases, is paid by the host, and guests no longer pay a separate service fee. For hosts in the European Economic Area, Italy included, the switch began on 13 October 2026.

Before, the host paid about 3% and the guest paid 12% to 14% on top of the price. In practice, to keep the same net income, prices on Airbnb now need to be recalculated.

Booking.com

Booking.com charges a commission to the host only, usually between 10% and 25%, averaging around 15%. It is calculated on the total booking value, extras included, and invoiced monthly. Programmes that boost visibility (such as Preferred Partner or Genius discounts) usually involve extra costs or discounts.

The strategy that works: several channels, one calendar

Listing on both increases occupancy, especially in low season. To do it safely you need:

  • a channel manager that syncs calendars and prices, to avoid double bookings;
  • consistent prices that account for the different commissions;
  • a direct channel (website and WhatsApp) for returning guests, with no commission.
