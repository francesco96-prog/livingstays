What does a co-host do, and how much does it cost?

Short-term renting pays, but it is a job: messages at all hours, check-ins, cleaning, prices to update, reviews to manage. A co-host takes care of it for you. Here is what they do and what it costs.

What a co-host does

  • Listing: photos, title, descriptions in several languages, publishing on Airbnb, Booking.com and other channels.
  • Pricing: dynamic rates updated for season, events and demand.
  • Guests: quick replies, instructions, check-in (including self check-in), support during the stay.
  • Cleaning and linen: coordinating the teams and checking quality between guests.
  • Maintenance: small repairs and trusted technicians.
  • Paperwork: help with CIN and CIR, Alloggiati Web reporting, tourist tax, regular reports.

How much it costs: typical commissions

Service levelTypical commission
Channel and listing management only10–15%
Partial service15–20%
Full management20–30%
Luxury properties or remote areas30% and above

The most common model is a percentage of revenue; there are also fixed monthly fees or mixed formulas. Cleaning, photography and set-up costs are often charged separately.

Gross or net? The detail that changes everything

Two offers at “20%” can cost very different amounts. What matters is the basis of calculation:

  • on gross: the percentage applies to the full amount paid by the guest;
  • on net: it applies after platform commissions and any cleaning fees are deducted.

On a €1,000 booking with €155 Airbnb commission and €100 cleaning, 20% on gross is €200, on net €149. Always ask for it to be written into the contract.

What to check before signing

  1. The basis for the commission and which services are included.
  2. Who pays for cleaning, linen and consumables.
  3. Length of the contract, notice period and how to terminate.
  4. Access to your accounts and ownership of the listings (and their reviews).
  5. Reports: how often and with what data (revenue, occupancy, expenses).
  6. Who collects payments and how tax matters are handled.
