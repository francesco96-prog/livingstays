CIN and CIR: the rules for short-term rentals in Sicily

If you rent an apartment to tourists in Sicily, you need two codes: the CIN (national) and the CIR (regional). Without them your listing is not compliant, and fines can reach several thousand euros. Here is what you need, step by step.

The CIN: national identification code

The CIN was introduced by art. 13-ter of Decree-Law 145/2023 and is requested online through the BDSR, the Ministry of Tourism’s national database of accommodation. It applies to:

  • residential properties rented out for tourism;
  • units let as short-term rentals (up to 30 days);
  • hotels and other accommodation businesses.

The code must be shown in every online listing (Airbnb, Booking.com, your own website, social media) and displayed outside the building.

Safety: what you need to obtain it

To get the CIN, the property must meet the required safety standards: working gas and carbon monoxide detectors and compliant portable fire extinguishers, placed where they are easy to reach. The cost is modest, but it has to be done before you publish.

Fines

ViolationFine
Listing published without a CIN, or CIN never requested€800 to €8,000 per unit
CIN not displayed outside the building€500 to €5,000
No gas/CO detectors or extinguishers (business operators)€600 to €6,000

The CIR: Sicily’s regional identification code

The Sicilian Region introduced the CIR for all accommodation businesses and for tourist lets of less than 30 days, holiday homes included. You get it by registering the property on the regional tourism data platform (Turist@t).

The CIR must also appear next to the property name in listings, advertising and bookings, on every channel. The fine for not displaying it ranges from €500 to €5,000.

Other obligations not to forget

  • Reporting guests to the police (Alloggiati Web portal) within 24 hours of arrival.
  • Reporting statistics on arrivals and overnight stays to the Region.
  • Tourist tax, where the municipality applies it: it must be collected and paid according to local rules.
  • Tax return: rental income must be declared, and the property’s CIN must be stated in the return too.

Quick checklist

  1. Install gas/CO detectors and a fire extinguisher.
  2. Register the property on the regional platform and obtain the CIR.
  3. Request the CIN through the BDSR.
  4. Add the CIN and CIR to every listing and display the CIN outside.
  5. Set up Alloggiati Web and organise tourist tax collection.
