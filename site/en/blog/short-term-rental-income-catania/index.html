How much can you earn from a short-term rental in Catania?

“How much could I earn if I put my home on Airbnb?” It is the question we hear most. The honest answer: it depends, but market data gives a good starting point. And the gap between gross and net is bigger than most people think.

Catania’s market in numbers

According to Airbtics data on Airbnb listings in Catania (September 2025 – August 2026):

IndicatorValue
Average annual revenue per listingabout €21,000
Average daily rate (ADR)€87
Occupancy rate (median)65%
Active listings (October 2026)about 3,290
Strongest / weakest monthSeptember / January

These are averages: a well-located one-bedroom near the Cathedral, with good photos and high ratings, can do much better; an outlying property with a neglected listing, much worse.

From gross to net: an example

Take an apartment earning €21,000 gross in a year. The expense figures are indicative, only to show the reasoning:

ItemAmount
Gross revenue€21,000
Airbnb commission (15.5%)− €3,255
Cleaning and laundry (e.g. 80 stays × €35)− €2,800
Utilities, internet, consumables (estimate)− €1,800
21% flat tax on gross− €4,410
Net before maintenance and management≈ €8,700

If you hand management to a co-host, their commission also comes off. On the other hand, professional management usually raises both the average rate and occupancy: a few euros more per night and a few extra full weeks change the result more than any saving on expenses.

The levers that make the difference

  • Dynamic pricing: different rates by season, events (Sant’Agata, summer, long weekends) and booking lead time.
  • Photos and listing: the first photo and the title decide the click.
  • Reviews: high ratings improve your ranking in search results and your booking rate.
  • Multiple channels: Airbnb, Booking.com and direct bookings reduce empty periods.
  • Low season: mid-term stays for workers and students can fill January and February.
