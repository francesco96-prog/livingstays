Short-term rental tax in Italy in 2026: the 21% and 26% flat tax

Italy’s 2026 Budget Law confirmed the “two-speed” flat tax (cedolare secca) on short-term rentals and lowered the threshold above which renting counts as a business. Here is what changes if you rent out one or more homes.

The rules in brief

Properties let short-termTax regime
1st property (you choose which)flat tax 21%
2nd propertyflat tax 26%
From the 3rd property onbusiness activity: you need a VAT number (partita IVA)

The legal basis is art. 1, paragraph 17, of Law no. 199/2025 (2026 Budget Law). Previously, renting was presumed to be a business from the fifth property; now the third is enough.

When the flat tax applies

The short-term rental flat tax covers contracts of up to 30 days between private individuals, outside any business activity, for residential properties in Italy (cadastral categories A1 to A11, excluding A10) and their appurtenances. Services such as linen and cleaning are included in the rent.

The flat tax is optional: alternatively, the rent is added to your income and taxed under ordinary IRPEF rates. For medium to high incomes the flat tax is usually better value, but it should be assessed case by case.

A worked example

Two apartments each earning €20,000 a year, under the flat tax:

  • Apartment A (chosen as the first): 20,000 × 21% = €4,200
  • Apartment B: 20,000 × 26% = €5,200

So choosing the higher-earning home as your “first property” saves money: 5 percentage points on your most profitable property.

The role of platforms and intermediaries

When an intermediary (a platform or a property manager) collects the rent on the owner’s behalf, it applies a withholding tax on account and issues a certificate for it: in your tax return it is deducted from the tax due. Always keep the certificates you receive.

What to do now

  1. Count how many properties you actually let short-term.
  2. If you have two, decide with your accountant which one to declare at 21%.
  3. If you have three or more, plan ahead for opening a VAT number and choosing the right regime.
  4. Keep income, commissions and expenses in order: you need them both for your tax return and to understand your real returns.

This article is for information only and does not replace advice from an accountant or a lawyer: rules and rates change, so always check your own situation.
