About

Sicilian hospitality, a contemporary eye

Living Stays was born in Sicily from the idea that a short rental can have the soul of a home and the care of a boutique hotel.

We select a few properties, each with a clear character, and look after every detail: from interiors to welcome, from local tips to experiences. Today we are in Sicily — Catania, Porto Empedocle and Enna — and soon in Veneto.

Alongside Living Stays there is Living Experiences, our experiences brand: because a journey is remembered for what you live.
